What IGNIS measures

Five pillars. One score.

01 · Velocity

Shipped work per session

Did the vault actually move this week? Features, content, decisions, playable builds. No "planning sprints."

02 · Learning

Loops closed per session

Self-improvement entries, playtest signals integrated, audits acted on. Learning without follow-through doesn't count.

03 · Focus

Focus discipline

Always knowing the next most important thing, and keeping every project consistent with the others. Focus is a muscle that atrophies fast.

04 · Truth

Drift between plan and reality

If the site says one thing and the code says another, IGNIS drops. Transparency is not a slogan.

05 · Compound

Reuse and ecosystem gravity

Shared tooling across projects, cross-surface handoffs, one vault — not ten islands.

⚡ Forge Forecast · proprietary

What the vault sees coming.

IGNIS reads activity patterns + stated focus across the studio and projects two forward-looking signals: what's likely to ship soon, and what's climbing fastest. Confidence scores are transparent functions of observed studio activity — not opinion.

Reading forward-looking signals…

Predictions move with the published feed, not with a calendar. A "Likely to ship" card doesn't promise a date — it reads the pre-launch shape of a project's stated focus and output volume, and surfaces it. Read it as the vault's own forecast, not a roadmap.

How the Oracle is wired

One feed. One synthesis layer. No hidden opinion layer — just IGNIS's read.

Every VaultSpark project writes down what changed each time work ends. IGNIS turns those notes into public Oracle feeds showing each project's health, focus, and friction. The Oracle serves that read directly to your browser. No middleware. No hidden opinion layer. Just the studio's own ledger, made legible.

Voice quotations are IGNIS's own observations per project, refreshed on demand. The block on every project page is the same widget — same feed, same logic. One Oracle. Every surface.

Why we publish it

Radical transparency isn't a brand move. It's a constraint.

Most studios hide their dashboards. We publish ours because accountability is the cheapest way to stay honest. When the score is public, you can't fudge a session. You can't call a bad week "strategic." You ship, or the number drops, and the vault knows.

IGNIS is not a KPI we optimize for. It's a thermometer — a signal that we're either hot, warming, or cooling. Games get built when the forge stays hot. Membership is how you sit close to the fire.
