What IGNIS measures

Five pillars. One score.

01 · Velocity

Shipped work per session

Did the vault actually move this week? Features, content, decisions, playable builds. No "planning sprints."

02 · Learning

Loops closed per session

Self-improvement entries, playtest signals integrated, audits acted on. Learning without follow-through doesn't count.

03 · Focus

Runway discipline

Pre-loaded Now queue, no runway crashes, cross-repo coherence. Focus is a muscle that atrophies fast.

04 · Truth

Drift between plan and reality

If the site says one thing and the code says another, IGNIS drops. Transparency is not a slogan.

05 · Compound

Reuse and ecosystem gravity

Shared tooling across projects, cross-surface handoffs, one vault — not ten islands.

Why we publish it

Radical transparency isn't a brand move. It's a constraint.

Most studios hide their dashboards. We publish ours because accountability is the cheapest way to stay honest. When the score is public, you can't fudge a session. You can't call a bad week "strategic." You ship, or the number drops, and the vault knows.

IGNIS is not a KPI we optimize for. It's a thermometer — a signal that we're either hot, warming, or cooling. Games get built when the forge stays hot. Membership is how you sit close to the fire.
