Short Bio (150 words)

VaultSpark Studios is an independent creative studio building browser-based games, cinematic worlds, creative tools, and AI-native intelligence under a single operating philosophy — the vault. Every project is forged in the open, sharpened under iteration, and released only when it's charged enough to spark; some are later vaulted again to hold their charge until the spark returns. Free to play, instant in the browser, no download required.

Founded in March 2026, the studio now spans 27 initiatives across games, platforms, and tools. Six are sparked — Call of Doodie, Franchise Architect, PromoGrind, Velaxis, Vorn, and VEILOS — with fourteen more in active forge across games, platforms, and tools, including MindFrame, Solara, Voidfall, The Exodus, Seamline, Hashmark, SHADOW, Concurrent, and Syntha. A cross-project membership platform called the Vault binds every title through a free 9-tier rank system, optional VaultSparked subscriptions, and a shared points economy powered by 16 live edge functions.

Community Platform

The Vault Member System

VaultSpark Studios operates a cross-game membership platform called the Vault. A free account follows players across every studio title — tracking Vault Rank, achievements, challenge completions, and early access drops. The 9-tier rank system runs from Spark Initiate to The Sparked (100,000+ points), rewarding long-term engagement with increasingly exclusive access.

Optional paid tiers (VaultSparked from $4.99/mo; VaultSparked Eternal from $29.99/mo) add premium benefits including exclusive lore drops, Discord role sync, and Phase 1 grandfather pricing locked for early members. The Vault is the connective tissue binding every VaultSpark title into a single, expanding universe.
