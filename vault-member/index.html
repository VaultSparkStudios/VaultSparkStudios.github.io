Studio Membership  ·  Free Forever

Enter
The Vault.

One account. Every world we build. Your rank, your handle, your identity — carried across every game and tool in the VaultSpark ecosystem. We're just getting started.

Obelisk creates and protects your one VaultSpark identity. Passkeys, authenticator codes, recovery, sessions, and security receipts live there. Your free Vault Membership profile, handle, rank, and plan stay here.

Create your Obelisk account →

Obelisk opens on its sign-in screen. If you are new, choose “New here? Create your account” there. New Obelisk accounts are invite-led right now — if the Gate asks for an invite, request one here and we will reply from a VaultSpark address. Either way you return here to finish your Vault profile.

  • No VaultSpark password is created or stored.
  • Obelisk proves who you are; Vault Membership decides what member features you receive.
  • Your existing member history is preserved by verified email and UUID continuity.

Already a member? Sign in

Vault Membership remains free. Identity security is provided by Obelisk; membership benefits and rank are provided by VaultSpark.

Sign in through Obelisk. VaultSpark never receives your passkey or authenticator secret. After Obelisk verifies you, this portal restores your existing member UUID, rank, subscriptions, and activity.
