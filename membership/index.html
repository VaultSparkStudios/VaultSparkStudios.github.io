Questions

Membership, answered

Do I need to pay to play VaultSpark games?

No. Every VaultSpark game is free to play, and no tier locks a game. Paid tiers add identity, Ask IGNIS, member drops and points bonuses.

How do I get a free Vault Member account?

New accounts are opened by invitation for now. Request your Vault invite, or use an invite link from a member, then create your account in the Vault Member portal.

How do I upgrade to VaultSparked or VaultSparked Eternal?

Sign in to your Vault Member portal and open the upgrade panel. Checkout runs through Stripe, and the new tier appears on your account once payment confirms.

Is there an annual plan?

Not yet. Every tier is billed monthly today. Annual plans are coming later, and this page will say so when they are on sale.

When do the bonus Vault Points arrive?

On each monthly renewal: 500 points for VaultSparked and 1,000 points for VaultSparked Eternal. The bonus starts with your first renewal, not on the day you subscribe.

What is the Phase 1 price lock?

Pricing is phased. If you join a tier during Phase 1, you keep its Phase 1 monthly price while your subscription stays active. Cancelling, a lapsed payment or switching tier ends the lock. The full wording is in the Terms of Service.

How do I cancel?

Open Manage billing in your Vault Member portal settings. Cancelling stops the next charge; your tier stays active until the end of the month you already paid for.

Can I gift a membership?

Not right now. Gift memberships are paused while we rebuild them properly.
