#### **1. Decouple the static-content release train from identity readiness** *(agent-doable today, no credential needed)*

**Why this is the root fix, not the hotfix lane.** A hotfix lane is an escape hatch that must be hand-driven, hand-scoped, and hand-verified every time; it re-derives its own blast radius per invocation and got it wrong once already. **Guard against the obvious failure:** the content lane must resolve every asset reference *through* the tree it is actually deploying (the S294 lesson), and `check-content-lane-purity` must enumerate **git-tracked** paths, never walk the filesystem (the CI-divergence lesson).
