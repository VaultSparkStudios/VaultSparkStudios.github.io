# The Vault Lifecycle — VaultSpark Studios Ecosystem Vocabulary

This is the studio's own coined vocabulary for how every creation moves through the ecosystem. It is one workflow, named with intention. **There is no "Sealed" status — what "sealed" means is VAULTED.**

---

## THE VAULT — the whole ecosystem (the umbrella)

**The Vault** is everything the studio holds: every game, cinematic world, creative tool, and AI-native intelligence it has conceived, is building, has launched, or has sealed. It is the umbrella term — **the Vault contains the Forge, the Sparked, and the Vaulted.** When we say "in the Vault," we mean anywhere in the ecosystem.

## ⚒️ FORGE — actively being built *(orange)*

The **Forge** is the furnace. It's where the studio's AI agents and the founder hammer raw concepts into real, studio-quality work — forging, upgrading, refining, improving. *(Visual metaphor: flames in a furnace, sparks flying as the work is shaped — rendered premium and refined, never literal/cartoonish.)* A project **in the Forge** is under active development.

## 🔥 SPARKED — released and live, still improving *(gold)*

A **Sparked** project has been released to the world and is live — **and is still under consistent improvement, with full studio support, backing, and focus.** Sparked does not mean "finished." It means launched *and* actively carried: the studio keeps shipping into it.

## 🔒 VAULTED — sealed, or a charged concept *(steel)*

**Vaulted** covers two kinds of thing:

1. **A brand-new concept** within the studio ecosystem that is **not yet** in the Forge or Sparked — conceived, named, holding its charge, but not yet being worked on. (The charged backlog.)
2. **A project that was Sparked or in the Forge** that the founder has decided to **seal / lock / cage / store / pause** — temporarily or permanently. Reasons are the founder's to set: it wasn't a great studio fit; it couldn't reach the studio-quality standard; a better idea absorbed or replaced it; or any reason the founder deems appropriate. Where a project was **renamed or rebranded**, a **tombstone** marks the old/dead name so history stays honest.

**Vaulted is never "gone."** A vaulted project holds its charge and can always reignite.

## The cycle is reversible

```
        ┌─────────────── UNVAULT ───────────────┐
        ▼                                        │
   VAULTED ──(concept)──▶ FORGE ──(release)──▶ SPARKED ──(founder seals)──▶ VAULTED
        ▲                                                                       │
        └───────────────────────── (founder seals, any time) ──────────────────┘
```

- **UNVAULT** — a Vaulted project returns to the **Forge** to be worked on again.
- **RESPARK** — a project that was Vaulted after being live is brought back through the Forge and **Sparked again** (a *Respark*; or simply Sparked).
- **Tombstones** — permanent markers for old rebrands / dead project names; they keep the lineage honest even when the name is gone.

## Usage

- Statuses, in our words: **Forge · Sparked · Vaulted** (umbrella: **the Vault**). Never "Live" for Sparked; never "Sealed" as a separate status.
- Verbs: *forge it · spark it · vault it · unvault it · respark it.*
- This lifecycle is the studio's public, honest map of how work flows — surfaced for the world on **`/atlas/`** (the ecosystem map) and held as studio vocabulary everywhere.
