# Audit — 2026-08-08 (S308)

**Scope:** The Desk (`/news/`) editorial engine, in response to the founder's directive:
*"Are those the best editorial personas? How do we create even more engaging personalities and voices and commentary, and use even more relevant trending, viral, high-searched content/categories to consistently generate new reports throughout every single day (and have a CTA for a newsletter where it sends out via Brevo or anything we can build ourselves)?"*

**Method:** read the live code (`scripts/lib/news-desk.mjs`, `build-news-desk.mjs`, `generate-news-pages.mjs`), the committed corpus (`data/news-desk/days/2026-08-07.json`), the Brevo account state, and the Supabase function inventory. Every finding below was verified against live code or a live service, not inferred from documentation.

---

## Ranked findings

### 1 — The cast is one-dimensional *by construction* (P0, shipped)

`stance.direction` is a single scalar in `[-2,2]` and `computeHeat` is the confidence-weighted mean pairwise distance on that one number. The debate axis **is** hype level. REX (up), MARA (careful), DOT (down) are therefore three points on one line, and every story resolves to the same argument shape.

This is the root cause of the founder's instinct that the personas could be better — the personas are well-written; the *space they argue in* has one dimension. Adding a fourth opinion about hype would have made the sameness worse.

**Fix shipped:** a second axis `horizon` (-2 immediate … +2 structural), 2-D heat, and `heatBreakdown()` naming the shape (`split-on-worth` / `split-on-timing` / `split-on-both` / `aligned`). Backward compatibility is structural: `horizon` defaults to 0 and the divisor stays at 4 (the 1-D maximum), so published heat provably cannot move.

### 2 — Voice depth was one sentence per persona (P0, shipped)

Each persona carried a single `voice` string. No lexicon, no signature move, no forbidden move, no relationships — so "more engaging commentary" had nothing to hang on.

**Fix shipped:** full voice specs (`beats`, `lexicon`, `signature`, `forbidden`, `rival`) plus three new personas differentiated by **what they know**, not by mood: VERA (has run it in production), ECHO (has seen the cycle), JUNO (tracks who it lands on). `castForStory()` seats a beat-owning anchor and its declared rival so six voices create rotation, not noise.

### 3 — The public track record never fed back into the writing (P0, shipped)

`personaTrackRecords()` already graded every persona from a hash-chained ledger, and nothing read that grade back. The personas could never change.

**Fix shipped:** `personaForm()` converts ledger accuracy into a writing directive — chastened on a cold streak, emboldened on a hot one — gated at four resolved calls so a thin record earns `unproven` and no tone shift. This is the single most defensible differentiator: it is only possible because the desk keeps a verifiable record.

### 4 — Intraday publishing was structurally impossible (P0, shipped)

`validateDay()` hard-capped 1–3 stories per day and the model was day-atomic, so "throughout every single day" could not be expressed at all.

**Fix shipped:** `EDITIONS` (Wire 06:00 · Midday · Close · Late Night), each with its own editorial job and cap. Volume discipline moves from per-day to per-edition, so cadence rises without any edition becoming a link dump.

### 5 — There was zero trend sourcing (P0, shipped)

`--rebuild` consumed only hand-committed days. Cadence was bounded by how fast a human found stories.

**Fix shipped:** `news-trend-radar.mjs` + `lib/news-trends.mjs` (56 self-tests). Free, key-less sources only (CANON-029). Corroboration across independent outlets is the largest scoring term; engagement — the signal most likely to reward slop — is capped at 15. Four hard disqualifications: single unverified source, already-covered re-run, uncastable beat, vendor marketing.

### 6 — Two sourcing bugs found by running it, not reading it (P0, shipped)

- **Corroboration was silently dead.** Every Google News RSS link is a `news.google.com` redirect, so `sourceDomain()` returned `google.com` for a hundred independent outlets. The highest-weighted signal could never fire, and the symptom was merely a thin queue.
- **Vendor marketing scored as news.** Lab blogs publish customer case studies through the same feed as announcements.

Both root-fixed with regression tests. Live queue: **7 → 24 queued**, with real multi-source corroboration.

### 7 — The Desk had no newsletter path (P0, shipped)

`send-member-newsletter` exists but is gated behind Vault Member / Obelisk. The Desk's entire product claim is that it needs no account, so that function structurally cannot serve `/news/`.

**Fix shipped:** `subscribe-desk-dispatch` — identity-free, Brevo double opt-in, origin-allowlisted, enumeration-safe. Live-verified 5/5 including negative controls.

### 8 — Persona count was hardcoded in public copy (P1, shipped)

The hub read "Three minds" and "three named editorial lenses" as literals. A roster change would have made a public page lie.

**Fix shipped:** all counts derive from `PERSONAS.length`.

### 9 — Pre-existing: the genius-list generator contradicted itself (P1, shipped)

`rationaleFor()` wrote "requires founder sign-off" into BRAND item rationales while `actionable` was computed from task text alone. The gate-integrity check reads both and correctly failed. Debt from S307's closeout — the offending item was written to TASK_BOARD *after* S307's build check ran.

**Fix shipped:** the gate derives from the category that produces the prose, so generator and validator cannot disagree. The check was right and was not softened.

## Deferred, with reason

- **Radar → authored edition.** The radar produces a ranked, edition-assigned queue; turning a queued topic into a `validateDay()`-clean day is still manual. Deferred rather than faked: auto-generating a day would have risked simulated content entering the public corpus, which D-S307.2 forbids structurally.
- **Scheduling the radar.** Needs the drafting path first, or a cron would produce queues nobody consumes.
- **Anthropic/Meta first-party feeds.** Both 404 — no public RSS exists. Coverage of them arrives via aggregator queries instead, and `sourceHealth` reports reachability honestly rather than letting an unreachable source read as a quiet news day.
