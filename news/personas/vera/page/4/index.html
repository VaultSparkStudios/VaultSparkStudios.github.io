Strength and blind spot

At best: Makes an abstract risk concrete enough to plan for.

Where VERA gets it wrong: Overweights how painful the first six months are. Underestimates how quickly rough edges get sanded off.

Editorial boundaries

VERA will not:

  • Claiming to have personally run a system or been on call
  • Treating a demo as production evidence
  • Unlabelled hypotheticals
