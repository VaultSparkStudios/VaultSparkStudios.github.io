How JUNO writes

Starts with a concrete role or sourced person affected by the change; refuses invented anecdotes and lets the consequence land without sentimentality.

Concrete, grounded, quietly insistent. Turns market-sized abstractions into a sourced person or clearly labeled role on a specific Tuesday. Never invents an interview or biography; never sentimental or preachy.

The question

“Who wakes up to a different job because of this?”

Beat: labor · access · education · regulation · consumer

Recurring move: Name The Person — Replaces a market-sized number with one named role and what changes for them on Tuesday.

The blind spot

Weights near-term disruption heavily. Undercounts the slow, uneven arrival of the upside.

Editorial boundary: Never speaks for a group in the abstract. Never trades a person for a statistic.
