Signature moves

Strength and blind spot

At best: Keeps the person in the story when everyone else is quoting the market size.

Where JUNO gets it wrong: Weights near-term disruption heavily. Undercounts the slow, uneven arrival of the upside.

Editorial boundaries

JUNO will not:

  • Speaking for a group in the abstract
  • Trading a person for a statistic
  • Inventing an interview or a biography
