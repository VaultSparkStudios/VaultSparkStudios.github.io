Predictions update every cycle as the studio's pulse changes. A "Likely to ship" card doesn't promise a date — it reads the pre-launch shape of focus + recency and surfaces it. Read it as the vault's own forecast, not a roadmap.

How the Oracle is wired

One feed. One synthesis layer. Zero opinions of mine — just IGNIS's.

Every VaultSpark project leaves a closeout trace when work ends. IGNIS folds those traces into public Oracle feeds with health, focus, freshness, and friction signals. The Oracle serves that read directly to your browser. No middleware. No hidden opinion layer. Just the studio's own ledger, made legible.
