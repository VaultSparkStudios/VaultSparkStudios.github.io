Brand  ·  Assets  ·  Usage

Brand Kit.

Official VaultSpark Studios logo, colors, and usage guidance. Direct-copy URLs for partners, journalists, and creators. All assets are proprietary — usage for coverage or partnership is permitted; modification, recoloring, or commercial re-use without permission is not.

Writing about us? The full Press Kit → adds studio facts, key art, and contacts.

Asset Library

Logos

Every variant is served as both WebP (modern, ~6× smaller) and PNG (Outlook-safe). Click Copy URL to hotlink; click Download to save. Manifest JSON: /brand/assets.json.

Email

Email Signature Logo

The signature-optimized PNG is 400×400 pixels, transparent background, ~240KB — ideal for Gmail, Zoho, Outlook, and Apple Mail. In your email client's signature editor, use the "image from URL" option and paste:

https://vaultsparkstudios.com/assets/brand/logo-signature.png
Download Download @2x (retina)

Recommended display width: 160–200px. The @2x variant renders crisper on high-DPI displays but is larger (~830KB); either works in most clients.

Color System

Palette

Click any swatch to copy its hex value. These are the core tokens used across the site; individual project brands may add accents (e.g. Call of Doodie orange, Solara sunset).

Usage

Guidelines

  • Do: use the logo in press coverage, partner pages, podcast artwork, and accurate attribution.
  • Do: maintain clear space equal to the sigil's width on all sides.
  • Do not: recolor, stretch, rotate, apply effects, or crop the sigil out of the wordmark.
  • Do not: imply endorsement of third-party products, services, or views.
  • Do not: use in a way that could confuse the reader about the source of the content.
