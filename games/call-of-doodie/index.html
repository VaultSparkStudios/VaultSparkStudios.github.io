Chaos Is The Mechanic.

Call of Doodie is a comedy-first top-down roguelite shooter where absurdity is the primary mechanic and punchlines arrive via ammunition. Survive endless waves of absurd enemies using increasingly ridiculous weapons — and every run plays different thanks to archetype-driven perk builds, challenge modes, seeded runs, and daily missions.

  • Fast-paced arcade shooter gameplay — instant in your browser, no download required
  • Comedy-first design — every encounter is a setup for the punchline
  • Active development with live updates and content additions
  • Cross-game progress tracking with a free Vault Member account
  • No paywall, no install, no account required to start playing
