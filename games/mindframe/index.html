MindFrame

Get Early Signal All Games

Make Your Own Thinking Visible.

MindFrame is a metacognition training platform for curious, high-agency adults. Every session is a mirror: what you predicted, what you actually did, where your confidence was wrong, and what your mind looks like today versus last week. You don't just play — you watch yourself think, and you get sharper for it.

Cognitive skill tools like Lumosity, Elevate, and BrainHQ train isolated drills without teaching metacognition — the skill of knowing how you think. Users plateau and churn because they lack a model of their own cognition. MindFrame makes the user's own thinking observable, measurable, and trainable.

  • 15 core cognitive modes across Foundation, Advanced, and Elite tiers
  • 620+ hand-crafted and AI-generated challenges — never a static library
  • Mind Model: a persistent per-user cognitive profile that adapts challenge selection
  • Adaptive AI loop — grounded in user state, cached, measured; not a chatbot wrapper
  • Honest paywalls, frictionless churn, zero dark patterns
  • Built on Next.js 15, NestJS, Supabase Postgres, Upstash, Anthropic, Stripe

Status: Live in production, public-unlaunched — access is beta-outreach gated. Join the Vault for invite access.
