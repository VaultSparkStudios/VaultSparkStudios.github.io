Frequently Asked Questions

Are VaultSpark games free to play?

Yes. All live VaultSpark games are completely free to play in your web browser. No download, no installation, no paywall. A free Studio Member account unlocks additional features and cross-game tracking.

What kinds of games does VaultSpark make?

VaultSpark Studios makes browser-based games across multiple genres: action comedy (Call of Doodie), football franchise sim (Gridiron GM), NFL front-office sim (Franchise Architect), real-time strategy (VaultFront), and roguelite RPG (Solara: Sunfall). Every game is built to feel cinematic, immersive, and unmistakably part of the VaultSpark portfolio.

What's the difference between Gridiron GM and Franchise Architect?

Gridiron GM is a session-based front-office management sim set in a fictional NFL-style universe — pick a team, draft players, manage the cap, and watch your season simulate with animated play-by-play. There's no on-field gameplay; you're the GM calling shots from the front office. Franchise Architect is a persistent simulation with saved leagues, multi-season career arcs, and a full franchise hub you can return to across sessions.

Can I play VaultSpark games on my phone?

VaultSpark games are web-first experiences that run in modern browsers. While most games work on mobile devices, the best experience is on desktop. Mobile optimization varies by title — check each game's page for details.

How do I get early access to games in development?

Create a free Vault Member account to join the early-access pool for upcoming releases. VaultSparked members get first-wave priority when invites, beta keys, and closed builds go live.

Will VaultSpark games ever come to Steam or mobile app stores?

VaultSpark's current focus is on web-first browser experiences. Console and mobile app distribution may come for select titles as the studio grows. Follow the studio's channels for announcements.
