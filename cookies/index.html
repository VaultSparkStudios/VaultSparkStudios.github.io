Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies VaultSpark Studios uses on vaultsparkstudios.com, what they do, how long they last, and how you can manage or disable them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit a website. Browser storage technologies like localStorage and sessionStorage serve a similar purpose — storing small amounts of data locally to improve your experience.

Third-Party

We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or behavioural targeting technologies. No third-party cookies are set beyond those listed above.

Managing Cookies

Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings. Note that blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the site from functioning correctly.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Google Analytics Opt-Out

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser settings.

Clearing Local Storage

To clear localStorage data set by VaultSpark Studios, open your browser's developer tools (usually F12), navigate to the Application or Storage tab, and clear the entries for vaultsparkstudios.com.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our technology or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
